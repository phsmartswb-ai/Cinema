Our Story

Thirty
Years of
Cinema

"We did not open a theatre. We built a sanctuary for those who believe a great film can change who you are."

In 1994, founder Vikram Nair opened the first Lumière auditorium in Bandra with a single conviction: that Indian audiences deserved to experience cinema the way its creators imagined it — with perfect light, perfect sound, and perfect silence around them.

Three decades, three cities, and fifteen screens later, that conviction remains unchanged. Every seat, every speaker, every dimming of lights is an act of devotion to the art of film.

— Vikram Nair, Founder & Chairman

Our Journey

1994

The First Curtain Rise

Lumière Bandra opens with two screens — Mumbai's first dedicated art-house cinema.

2001

Sound Reimagined

First cinema in India to install full Dolby Digital EX surround across all halls.

2009

Delhi & the Grand Expansion

Connaught Place flagship opens — six screens, the Director's Lounge, and fine dining.

2018

The IMAX Era

First independent cinema in South Asia to secure an IMAX with Laser installation.

2024

Hyderabad & the Future

Third city. D-Box motion seating. Dolby Vision. A spa. The most complete cinema experience in India.
